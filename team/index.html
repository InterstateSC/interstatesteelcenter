THE PEOPLE
BEHIND THE STEEL

A tightly knit team with deep industry experience, built over decades, unified by a commitment to precision, reliability, and service.

Executive Leadership

LEADERSHIP TEAM

BH
Brack Hudson
Chairman & Chief Executive Officer

Brack oversees the strategic direction, capital investment, and long term growth of Interstate Steel Center. Under his leadership, ISC has expanded its production capacity, modernized its equipment fleet, and added slitting capability, while maintaining the company's founding commitment to conflict free toll processing. He is deeply involved in equipment procurement, facilities, risk management, and operational decisions across every function of the business.

SV
Stephanie Valle
General Manager

Stephanie leads day to day operations across the facility, coordinating production scheduling, customer service, vendor relationships, and facility management. With deep knowledge of ISC's customer base and operational workflows, she serves as the primary point of contact for operational matters and ensures that the company's commitments to turnaround time and quality are met on every order.

MH
Mirely Hernandez
Operations Manager

Mirely oversees the coordination of production resources, equipment scheduling, and workflow across ISC's five lines. She works directly with production supervisors to ensure that orders are processed efficiently, equipment is properly utilized, and capacity is allocated to meet customer commitments. Her role bridges the strategic direction of the GM with the day to day realities of the production floor.

JH
Jennifer Hudson
Chief Financial Officer

Jennifer manages ISC's financial operations, including accounting, treasury, financing relationships, and financial planning. Her oversight ensures that capital investments are structured to support sustainable growth while maintaining the financial discipline that has kept ISC independent and family owned for over five decades. Jennifer works closely with the executive team on all major equipment and facility decisions.

Management

OPERATIONS & MANAGEMENT

MM
Mayra Medina
Accounting

Manages accounts receivable, accounts payable, payroll, and financial reporting. Mayra ensures the accuracy and timeliness of all financial transactions and works closely with the CFO on month end close and vendor management.

CA
Cinthia Aguayo
Inside Sales

Cinthia manages ISC's customer relationships, quoting, and new account development. She is the first point of contact for prospective customers and works with the operations team to ensure that sales commitments align with production capacity and scheduling realities.

JS
Jorge Sanchez
Production Supervisor

Jorge supervises production line operations, ensuring that orders are run to specification, equipment is properly set up, and tolerances are maintained. He coordinates directly with the Operations Manager to manage daily production sequencing and output quality.

CB
Cuco Barriga
Production Supervisor

Cuco brings hands on production expertise to ISC's leveling and slitting operations, overseeing line setup, crew performance, and safety compliance. His experience on the production floor ensures consistent output quality and efficient throughput across his assigned lines.

FD
Fausto Delgado
Shipping & Receiving Supervisor

Fausto manages all inbound coil receiving and outbound finished goods shipping, coordinating carrier logistics, load verification, and documentation accuracy. His oversight of the dock ensures that material is received correctly and that finished orders ship on time and to customer packaging requirements.

JM
Jose Molina
Inventory Manager

Jose manages ISC's inventory tracking systems, ensuring that every coil and finished bundle is accurately recorded, located, and traced from receipt through delivery. He maintains heat number traceability across all customer material and supports real time inventory visibility for customer inquiries.

CV
Christian Valdez
QC Inspection

Christian inspects processed material against customer specifications before it ships, verifying flatness, cut length, squareness, and slit width tolerances on every order. His attention to detail is the final checkpoint that ensures each coil and sheet leaving ISC meets the precision standards our customers depend on.

JF
Juan Favella
Shipping & Receiving Desk

Juan coordinates the shipping and receiving desk, processing inbound coil arrivals and outbound order paperwork so material moves through the dock without delay. He keeps carrier schedules, bills of lading, and delivery documentation accurate, making sure every load is recorded correctly and dispatched on time.
