ISC coil and sheet storage, secure traceable staging for material we process in Los Angeles.

Interstate Steel Center stores your coil and finished sheet as part of our toll processing service in Los Angeles, California. We are not a public warehouse. We hold the material we level and slit for you, with full mill certification and heat number traceability, so it stays staged, tracked, and ready to process or ship on your schedule.

Storage that comes with processing

Storage at ISC is tied to processing. You stage coil and finished product with us when we are the ones leveling or slitting it, which keeps your material under one roof from arrival through processing to shipment. We do not take in coil for warehousing alone, and that is by design: it keeps your material moving and keeps us focused on the processing you came to us for.

Built for steel

Holding flat rolled steel well is about traceability and condition, not just floor space. ISC tracks every coil and bundle from receipt through delivery, retains mill test reports and heat numbers, and keeps material indoors and organized so it reaches the next operation in the same condition it arrived.

What you get

  • Heat number traceability and mill cert retention on every coil and bundle
  • Real time inventory visibility, so you always know what is on hand
  • Secure, organized indoor storage in our 107,000 sq. ft. Los Angeles facility
  • Process on demand: leveled or slit and shipped with next day turnaround

Stage your West Coast material with the processor that runs it

ISC processes and stages heavily for brokers: both West Coast brokers serving the local market and brokers across the country who want a West Coast presence without operating a facility here. For any broker, mill, or service center, we can level or slit your coil and then hold the finished product steps from the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach. Rail coil west, let us process it, and have us stage it for release to your customers on local lead times. As tariffs reshape where steel flows, a processing and staging partner on the West Coast is a real edge.

Why stage with an independent

ISC never sells steel. The material we process and hold for you sits with a partner who runs it, not a competitor who might rather sell their own. Zero channel conflict, full visibility, and your product ready when you are.
