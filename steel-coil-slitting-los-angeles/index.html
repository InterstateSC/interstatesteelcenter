What is steel coil slitting?

Slitting takes a wide master coil and runs it through rotary knives that cut it lengthwise into multiple narrower coils of an exact width. It's how a 60-inch master becomes the precise mults a stamper, roll former, or tube mill actually runs, with clean edges and tight, repeatable width control.

Edge quality and width consistency are everything downstream. Our newest line is built for precision: tight tolerances, minimal burr, and consistent coil set from the first wrap to the last.

High capacity precision slitting

Our slitting line is ISC's newest investment, expanding the company from a leveling specialist into full service flat rolled processing under one roof in central Los Angeles.

Slitting capacity & tolerances

  • Master coils up to 50,000 lbs; entry OD to 80"
  • Gauge range 10 to 30 ga.; widths to 60"
  • Up to 23 slit cuts per pass; minimum slit width 1.5"
  • Width tolerance to ±.005"; side trim from .25" per side

Why an independent slitter

Nearly every slitter in Southern California also sells steel, so sending them your coil means feeding a competitor. ISC never sells steel. We slit only customer owned material, which makes us the natural overflow and precision slitting partner for brokers and service centers who refuse to route business to a rival.

Built for the West Coast market

We're minutes from the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach and rail accessible for inbound coil. As tariffs push up import costs, East Coast and Midwest mills and brokers are railing coil west to reach West Coast demand, and ISC slits it for them locally, so they serve the market without owning a plant here.
