Our Story

FOUNDED IN 1970

Interstate Steel Center was founded in 1970 at 7001 South Alameda Street in Los Angeles, a location the company has called home for over five decades. From its earliest days, ISC was built around a single, unwavering principle: provide the West Coast steel industry with a trusted, independent toll processing partner that would never compete with its own customers.

In an industry where many processors have drifted into distribution and steel sales, ISC has held the line. We process customer owned coils. We do not buy, inventory, or sell steel. This discipline has defined our relationships and driven our growth for more than 55 years.

Our 100,000 sq. ft. processing floor, Los Angeles, CA

What began as a single leveling line serving local service centers has grown into a 100,000 square foot facility housing four high capacity leveling lines and, most recently, a precision slitter, expanding ISC's capabilities into full slit coil production for the first time in our history.

Today, ISC serves service centers, brokers, OEMs, and manufacturers across the western United States and beyond. Our customers range from regional distributors to some of the largest steel buyers in North America. They share a common expectation: when a coil leaves ISC, it is flat, square, and packaged to spec, every time.

Through five decades of changing markets, ownership has remained in the family, and so has the discipline that built the company. ISC's reputation rests not on how much steel we move, but on how precisely we return it.

Company History

FIVE DECADES OF PRECISION

1970
Founded in Los Angeles
Interstate Steel Center opens at 7001 South Alameda Street. The founding mission: serve the West Coast steel industry as a pure play toll processor, with no steel sales, ever. The first leveling line begins serving local service centers and distributors.
1980s
Capacity Expansion
Growing demand from regional service centers drives investment in additional leveling capacity. ISC expands its equipment lineup and customer base across the western United States, building a reputation for reliability and consistent tolerances larger processors couldn't match.
1990s
Heavy Gauge & Facility Growth
ISC invests in heavy gauge leveling, extending capability from light gauge coil into thicker plate and structural grades. The Alameda Street facility grows to its current 100,000 sq. ft. footprint, adding dedicated coil and sheet storage for a growing base of OEMs and national distributors.
2000s
Technology & Inventory Systems
ISC implements advanced inventory tracking software for real time coil and sheet visibility with full heat number traceability. The 72" leveling line is commissioned, extending width capability and attracting a new tier of large volume customers who require wide format processing.
2010s
Operational Excellence
Under consistent family ownership, ISC deepens relationships with major West Coast service centers and national OEM accounts. The packaging program of heat treated skids, custom branded covers, and heavy gauge banding is refined to meet the exacting requirements of automotive, construction, and heavy equipment end users.
2025
Slitting Line Installed: A New Chapter
ISC commissions its first precision slitter, running coils to 50,000 lbs, entry ODs to 80", and up to 23 slit cuts per pass at ±.005" tolerance. Slitting transforms ISC from a leveling only operation into a full service flat rolled processing center, while the founding commitment to toll only, conflict free service remains unchanged.
