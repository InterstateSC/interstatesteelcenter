The ISC Advantage

WE ARE NOT
YOUR COMPETITOR

ISC is the only toll processor on the West Coast that does not, and will not, sell steel. Your coils remain yours. Your customers remain yours. We return them leveled or slit to spec, and nothing more.

Service centers, brokers, and OEMs have relied on ISC for over five decades for exactly this reason: zero conflict of interest. We are a pure play processing partner. Full stop.

    Next Day Turnaround
